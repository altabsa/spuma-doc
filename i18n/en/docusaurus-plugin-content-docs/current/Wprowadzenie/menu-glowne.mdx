## Main Menu Structure

The main menu sections and their purpose are described below.

:::danger[]
The view includes all available sections, but depending on user permissions, some of them may be hidden.
:::

### Secretary

[This section](/i18n/en/docusaurus-plugin-content-docs/current/Sekretariat/sekretariat.md) is where document workflow begins. Unprocessed documents are added here.

### Search Results

A temporary section where results from the [global search](/i18n/en/docusaurus-plugin-content-docs/current/wyszukiwanie/wyszukiwanie_globalne.md) in the menu bar are displayed.

### My documents

A section related to user activity:

- [**Pending**](/i18n/en/docusaurus-plugin-content-docs/current/Moje/Oczekujace.md) – documents requiring your action
- [**Favorites**](/i18n/en/docusaurus-plugin-content-docs/current/Moje/Ulubione.md) – documents marked by you
- [**Recent**](/i18n/en/docusaurus-plugin-content-docs/current/Moje/Ostatnie.md) – recently opened documents
- [**Advice**](/i18n/en/docusaurus-plugin-content-docs/current/Moje/awizacja.md) – a section for adding Advice entries.

### Electronic Documents

Access to [electronic documents](/i18n/en/docusaurus-plugin-content-docs/current/dokumenty-elektroniczne/dokumenty-elektroniczne.md), such as KSeF invoices or documents from other integrated external sources.

### Repository

[The main document management section](/i18n/en/docusaurus-plugin-content-docs/current/Repozytorium/Repozytorium.md), storing documents in folders corresponding to document categories.

### Reports

Access to the [Reports section](/i18n/en/docusaurus-plugin-content-docs/current/Raporty/Raporty.md), including:

- Pending documents
- Document list
- Customer documents
- Access approvals

### Daily correspondence

Support for [Daily correspondence](/i18n/en/docusaurus-plugin-content-docs/current/Dzienniki.md) for incoming and outgoing correspondence.

### Trash

Contains documents deleted from the Repository. Depending on permissions, they can be restored or permanently deleted.

---

## Menu Behavior

- The menu can be **expanded and collapsed**.
- Menu sections can be **expanded and collapsed**.
- The view is dynamic and adapted to the user role – some sections may be hidden.
