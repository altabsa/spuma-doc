# Alerts

The **Alerts** section displays notifications related to documents and events in SPUMA.

## Viewing Alerts

1. Click the **bell icon** in the top-right corner to open the **Alerts** panel.
2. If alerts are available, they are displayed in the panel.
3. Expand an alert to view the related documents.
4. Click a document to **open it in a new tab** and view its details.
5. Click **Clear** to clear the displayed alerts.
6. Click **Refresh** to check for new alerts. **This is not required**, as alerts are refreshed automatically at regular intervals.
