# Electronic Documents

The **Electronic Documents** section allows you to work with documents that are imported into SPUMA through integrations with other systems.

This section includes, among others:

- documents from **[mailboxes](./skrzynka-pocztowa.md)**, e.g. invoices sent by e-mail,
- documents downloaded automatically from **[KSeF](./ksef.md)** (National e-Invoice System).

![Electronic Documents view](/img/dokumenty-elektroniczne_en.png)

---

## How to Use This Section?

1. In the Main Menu, expand the **Electronic Documents** category.
2. Click the required subsection, e.g. **Inbox** or **KSeF**.
3. The list displays documents transferred to SPUMA through the selected integration.
