# Inbox

The **Inbox** section in the **Electronic documents** menu allows you to receive and process e-mail messages together with their attachments.

E-mail messages may contain, for example, invoices that can then be converted into SPUMA documents and sent into workflow.

![Inbox view](/img/dokumenty-elektroniczne5_en.png)

---

## Filtering Messages

The following filters are available:

- **Only new** – displays unprocessed messages,
- **Only unread** – displays messages that have not yet been opened,
- **Filter field** – enter any phrase at the bottom of the list, e.g. sender e-mail address or part of the subject. After entering the phrase, confirm the search by pressing **Enter**, **Tab**, or clicking anywhere with the mouse.

---

## Message Preview

After clicking the selected message, its details are displayed:

- sender, date, and time,
- e-mail content,
- list of attachments.

![Message preview](/img/dokumenty-elektroniczne6_en.png)

Attachments can be opened in preview mode – clicking an attachment opens the document in a new tab.

---

## Adding a Document to Secretary

1. Click the **New document** button in the top-right corner.
2. A window opens where you can decide which content should be transferred to Secretary.

![New document from Inbox](/img/dokumenty-elektroniczne7_en.png)

---

### Adding Options

In the selection window, you can choose how to handle:

- **Content** of the message – add it as:
  - **Separate document**,
  - **Attach at beginning**,
  - **Attach at end**.

![New document from Inbox](/img/dokumenty-elektroniczne8_en.png)

- **Attachments** – they can be added to the document or skipped.

---

## Further Processing

After confirming the selection, the document or documents are added to **Secretary**, where their standard SPUMA workflow can be started.
