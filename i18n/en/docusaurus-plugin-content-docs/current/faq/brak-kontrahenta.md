# Business Partner Not Found in SAP – What to Do?

**Reported Issue**

When completing a document, the user cannot find the Business Partner in the data retrieved from SAP.

**Solution**

You do not need to open SAP and manually add the Business Partner.

1. Open the document and go to the **Attributes** tab.
2. Use the option to [**add a new Business Partner (BP)**](../przetwarzanie-pojedynczego-dokumentu/Atrybuty#adding-a-value).
3. The Business Partner created in SPUMA is also automatically added to SAP.
