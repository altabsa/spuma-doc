# Do I Have to Enter All the Details When Adding a New Business Partner?

**Reported Issue**

The user is concerned that adding a new Business Partner (BP) requires manually entering all Business Partner details.

**Solution**

You do not need to complete all fields.

1. Enter only the Business Partner's **Tax ID**.
2. Click **Online**.
3. The system automatically completes the remaining information.

For details, see [**Adding a Value**](../przetwarzanie-pojedynczego-dokumentu/Atrybuty.md#adding-a-value).
