# Do I Need to Send Emails Asking Who Approved a Document?

**Reported Issue**

The user wants to check who approved a document. They wonder whether they need to email other people to find out.

**Solution**

No.

In SPUMA, this information is available in the **decision history** on the **Flow** tab.

1. Open the document you want to check.
2. Go to the **Flow** tab.
3. Review the information about the authorization schema and the entire process.

The tab shows the complete decision history – who approved or rejected the document, when, and how.
