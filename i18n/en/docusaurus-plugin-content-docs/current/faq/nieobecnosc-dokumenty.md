# What Happens to My Documents When I Am Absent from Work?

**Reported Issue**

The user is concerned that documents will wait for their approval during their absence and that the approval process will stop.

---

**Solution**

No – in SPUMA, documents do not have to wait for a single person.

1. **Flow assigned to a user group**

   - If a document is assigned to a group, any member can approve it.
   - This allows the process to continue even when one person is absent.

2. **Flow assigned to an individual user**

   - In this case, the document waits for a decision from a specific person.
   - To keep the process moving, set up a substitute:

     1. Click your initials in the top-right corner and select [**Settings**](../konto_uzytkownika/ustawienia-uzytkownika.md).
     2. In the **During this period** section, specify the dates of your absence.
     3. In the **Replaced by** field, select the user who will make decisions on your behalf.
     4. Save your changes.
