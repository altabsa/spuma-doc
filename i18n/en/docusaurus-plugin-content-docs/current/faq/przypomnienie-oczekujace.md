# Will SPUMA Remind Me About Pending Documents?

**Reported Issue**

The user wants to know whether the system will remind them about documents awaiting their approval.

---

**Solution**

Yes – SPUMA reminds you about pending documents in several ways:

1. All documents requiring your decision are displayed in the **Pending** tab.
2. The **menu bar** at the top displays **notifications about documents awaiting approval**.
3. You can also enable **email notifications** to receive information about new documents directly in your inbox.
