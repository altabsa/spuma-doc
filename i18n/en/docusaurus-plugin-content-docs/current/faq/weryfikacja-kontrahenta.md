# Do I Need to Verify the Business Partner Name in SAP When Entering Its Code?

**Reported Issue**

The user wants to know whether they need to verify the Business Partner name in SAP when entering its code.

**Solution**

You do not need to verify the name in SAP – SPUMA is integrated with SAP and retrieves the data automatically.

1. Click the **double-square** icon next to the Business Partner (BP) field.
2. In the new window, search for the Business Partner in SAP:
   - Click the **search** icon to display all Business Partners.
   - Enter a search phrase to narrow the results.
3. Select the correct Business Partner from the list – the remaining Business Partner details are automatically retrieved from SAP.
