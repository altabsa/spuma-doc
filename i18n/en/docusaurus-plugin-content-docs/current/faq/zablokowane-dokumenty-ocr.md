# Documents Stuck in OCR – What to Do?

**Reported Issue**

The user reports that several documents are stuck in OCR processing and cannot be deleted.

---

**Solution**

1. Contact the **SPUMA administrator**.
2. The administrator can **withdraw documents from OCR** using the administration panel.
