# SPUMA

**Spuma** © is an advanced **DMS (Document Management System)**. Its main purpose is to manage, process, organize, and control document workflows within a company. It supports both paper and electronic documents, helping automate work and digitize office processes.

DMS-class systems are software solutions that support the management of documents created and received within organizations. Processing applies to both:

- **internal** documents, such as:
  - regulations,
  - requisitions,
  - HR department documents,
  - business trip requests and expense settlements,
  - certificates, policies, instructions,
- **external** documents:
  - customer orders,
  - incoming invoices,
  - traditional and electronic correspondence.

DMS systems support the collection, classification, and sharing of knowledge resources within an organization and provide greater control over document workflows.

Modern DMS applications, including SPUMA, use **OCR (Optical Character Recognition)** technologies to convert scanned documents into text, allowing them to be indexed, searched, and analyzed.
